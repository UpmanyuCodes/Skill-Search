How Skill Search works

Skill Search is a neighbourhood directory of verified local professionals. Customers find help nearby; skilled workers get steady local work without paying middlemen.

For customers

  1. Search by service, keyword (e.g. "geyser", "maths tuition"), city/pincode or your live location.
  2. Filter by rating, price, distance, emergency service and availability. See results on a map.
  3. Contact the way you like: phone call, WhatsApp, email, in-app chat or a booking request with your preferred date and time.
  4. Track bookings on your dashboard: pending, accepted, completed.
  5. Review the provider after the job — only real, completed bookings can be reviewed.

For service providers

  1. Register with your service, skills, experience, prices, working hours and the area you cover.
  2. Upload an ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Driving licence or Passport) for verification.
  3. Get verified by our team — only then does your profile appear in search.
  4. Receive requests, chat with customers, accept or decline jobs and mark them complete.
  5. Build reputation with ratings, and switch to "busy" whenever you're fully booked.

Verification & safety

  • Every provider must upload a government ID. We store only the last 4 characters of the ID number.
  • Admins review documents and approve or reject providers. Rejected providers can re-upload.
  • Phone numbers are shown only to logged-in users, and providers can hide them and use in-app chat only.
  • Anyone can report a profile for fake identity, fraud, behaviour or no-shows. Reports go straight to the admin team.
  • Reviews are tied to completed bookings, so ratings can't be faked by strangers.
